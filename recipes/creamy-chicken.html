Creamy Chicken

This is a recipe I found online, it is an easier modified verison of chicken au poivre and it has no business being this good. If you looking for somehting different to do with chicken then trust me this recipe is worth a try

Ingredients

  • 1 Whole chicken
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Coarsely grinded black pepper
  • 1 Shallot
  • 3 Cloves of garlic
  • 2 1/2 Cups heavy cream
  • 2 Pinches of salt
  • 2 tbsp Melted butter
  • 1 tbsp Rosemary
  • White cooking wine (I use a sauvignon blanc)

Steps

Getting the chicken started

  1. Start by spatchcocking the chicken, you can do it anyway you like. I cut from the front through the breast bone because its quick and easy.
  2. Salt the chicken all over, front and back.
  3. Rub the olive oil all over the chicken front and back.
  4. put the chicken in the oven at 375 degrees and let cook for 15-20 minutes.

Preparing the sauce

In a large mixing bowl combine:

  • 1 1/2 tbsp Coarsely grinded black pepper
  • 1 Shallot finely chopped
  • 3 Cloves minced garlic
  • 1 Pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp Melted butter
  • 2 1/2 Cups heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp Rosemary

Mix everything together until well combined.

Combining the the sauce with the chicken

  • After the chicken has cooked for 15-20 minutes at 375 degrees take it out and pour the sauce over the chicken and put it back in the oven at 400 degrees and let cook for about an hour.
  • Make sure to baste the chicken a minimum of two times while it is cooking.
