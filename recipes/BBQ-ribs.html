BBQ Ribs

Ingredients

This is a really simple recipe that is convenient for people like me who live in an apartment that won't allow the use of a BBQ, and gives a close enough taste of delicious BBQ ribs without using a BBQ.

  • Ribs
  • BBQ rub
  • Olive oil
  • BBQ Sauce
  • A good amount of tin foil

Steps

  1. Remove membrane on back of ribs with paper towel and a butter knife
  2. Coat ribs (front and back) with olive oil
  3. Rub in the BBQ rub, you don't have to go crazy with it just give it a good pat and make sure its on there.
  4. **IMPORTANT** This next step is the most important part of getting tender fall off the bone ribs without using a BBQ. wrap the rack of ribs tightly in tin foil, go around as many times as you would like, just make sure the ribs are completely covered and secured in the tin foil.
    If it leaks during the cooking process that is okay. The reason it is wrapped in tin foil is so that the ribs will cook and all of the fat juices that will come out will (mostly) stay with the ribs in the tin foil. Having the meat slowly cook in its own juices is the secret to getting that juicy tenderness.
  5. Place wrapped ribs on a cooking sheet and put in the oven at 275 degrees for at least 3 hours, the longer the better.
  6. After taking them out take the ribs out of the tin foil wrapping coat the ribs with BBQ sauce and put back in the oven on a low broil. Keep them in for about 10-15 minutes or until they start to looking good enough to eat.
