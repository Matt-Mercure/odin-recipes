Chicken Wings

This is a recipe on how to make chicken wings with a homemade BBQ seasoning. I use make this at home on a weekly basis because my wife and I really onjoy it. See ingredients and intrustions below.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Wings 10-15 per batch
  • 2tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1tsp Garlic powder
  • 1tsp Onion powder
  • 1tsp Old bay seasoning
  • 1tsp Black pepper
  • 2tsp sugar
  • 1 1/2tsp salt
  • 1/2tsp Cayenne pepper *Optional if you like it spicy
  • 1tsp Corn starch
  • 1tbsp Olive oil
  • 1-2 tbsp Plain greek yogurt

Steps

  1. Place all of the ingredients in a large mixing bowl and rub it on the chicken wings thoroughly. Let sit and marinate for at least 1 hour.
  2. place marinated wings on a baking tray and bake at 400 for 20-25 minutes flip wings hafway through. Make sure the internal temp is at least 165 degrees before taking out.
